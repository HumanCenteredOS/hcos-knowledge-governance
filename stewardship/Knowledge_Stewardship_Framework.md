# HCOS™ Knowledge Stewardship Framework

## Purpose

The HCOS™ Knowledge Stewardship Framework defines ongoing responsibility for preserving the usefulness, integrity, context, and continuity of HCOS knowledge.

## Stewardship Responsibilities

- maintain documents and links
- preserve provenance and decision history
- monitor evidence and implementation learning
- identify duplication, drift, contradiction, and gaps
- coordinate revision, deprecation, and archival work
- support succession and continuity
- protect meaning when knowledge moves across formats or repositories

## Stewardship Questions

- Is this artifact still accurate and useful?
- Is its intended scope clear?
- Are relationships and dependencies current?
- Has new evidence changed the interpretation?
- Are affected perspectives represented?
- What should future stewards understand?

## Principle

Stewardship is not passive storage. It is the continuing care of knowledge so that it remains trustworthy, understandable, and capable of serving people.
