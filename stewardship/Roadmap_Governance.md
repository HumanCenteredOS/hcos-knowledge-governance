# HCOS™ Roadmap Governance

## Purpose

This document defines how stewardship priorities are translated into a transparent HCOS development roadmap.

## Inputs

- architecture reviews
- evidence synthesis
- knowledge gaps
- implementation feedback
- repository health findings
- safety or dignity concerns
- dependency needs

## Priority Levels

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Exploratory

## Roadmap Record

Each item should identify purpose, evidence, affected artifacts, dependencies, owner, target milestone, and review status.

## Principle

The roadmap should reflect human consequence and architectural need, not novelty alone.
