# HCOS™ Knowledge Gap Assessment

## Purpose

This assessment identifies missing, weak, contradictory, or insufficiently mature areas within HCOS.

## Gap Types

- missing evidence
- missing affected perspectives
- unresolved contradiction
- absent Standard or Method
- Instrument without governing Method
- outdated learning resource
- unowned maintenance responsibility
- untested implementation assumption

## Prioritization

Evaluate gaps by:

- potential human consequence
- architectural dependency
- urgency
- evidence availability
- implementation demand
- effort and feasibility

## Output

Document the gap, why it matters, current evidence, recommended action, priority, owner, and review date.
