# HCOS™ Architecture Review Process

## Purpose

This process establishes periodic review of the HCOS ecosystem as a whole.

## Review Areas

- coherence and classification
- duplicate or conflicting concepts
- evidence maturity
- document dependencies
- terminology consistency
- repository navigation and health
- missing standards, methods, or instruments
- outdated or unused artifacts
- human impact and accessibility

## Outputs

- findings
- risks
- proposed merges, revisions, or retirements
- knowledge gaps
- priority roadmap
- assigned owners and review dates

## Cadence

Conduct reviews at meaningful milestones and at least annually once the ecosystem is in active public use. High-change disciplines may require more frequent review.
