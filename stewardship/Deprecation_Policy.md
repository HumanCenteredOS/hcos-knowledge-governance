# HCOS™ Deprecation Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how HCOS artifacts are withdrawn from recommended use while preserving history and transition guidance.

## Deprecation Reasons

- outdated evidence
- replacement by a stronger artifact
- material inconsistency with current architecture
- harmful or misleading application risk
- unmaintainable dependency
- redundancy

## Requirements

A deprecated artifact should display status, reason, effective date, replacement guidance, and archival location. Publicly referenced materials should not be silently deleted.
