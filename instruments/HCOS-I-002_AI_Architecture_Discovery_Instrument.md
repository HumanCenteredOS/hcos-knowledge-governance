# HCOS™ AI Architecture Discovery Instrument

## Purpose

Identify enduring human, organizational, systems, and architectural principles revealed by developments in AI.

## Instructions

Do not merely summarize the source. Distinguish temporary technology from durable systems lessons.

## Review Steps

1. Describe the event or development accurately.
2. Identify the immediate technical observation.
3. Identify the systems lesson.
4. Identify the organizational lesson.
5. Identify the human lesson.
6. Determine relevant HCOS domains, including Knowledge, Workflow, Resource, Governance, Reliability and Safety, Identity and Authority, Human Load, and Human Stewardship.
7. Assess evidence maturity.
8. Identify possible Standards, Methods, Instruments, Governance, Stewardship, Research, or Learning Resources.
9. State assumptions, contradictions, and unanswered questions.

## Output

- Source context
- Enduring principle
- Primary and secondary HCOS domains
- Human significance
- Evidence maturity
- Architecture opportunities
- Risks of premature generalization
- Recommended next action
