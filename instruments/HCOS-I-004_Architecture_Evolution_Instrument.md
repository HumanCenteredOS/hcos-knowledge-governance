# HCOS™ Architecture Evolution Instrument

## Purpose

Determine how new evidence or synthesized principles should evolve the HCOS architecture.

## Steps

1. State the enduring principle.
2. Identify the primary and secondary HCOS pillars affected.
3. Determine whether existing architecture should be strengthened, revised, merged, or expanded.
4. Evaluate opportunities for Foundations, Disciplines, Standards, Methods, Instruments, Governance, Stewardship, Research, and Learning Resources.
5. Identify dependencies, risks, and transition needs.
6. Prioritize recommendations as Critical, High, Medium, or Exploratory.
7. Identify validation and stewardship requirements.

## Output

- Executive Summary
- Enduring Principle
- Architectural Impact
- Recommended New or Revised Artifacts
- Dependencies
- Risks and Human Consequences
- Validation Plan
- Priority
- Roadmap Placement
- Review Date

## Guardrail

Prefer strengthening coherent existing architecture over creating unnecessary new artifacts.
