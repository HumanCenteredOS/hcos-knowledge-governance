# HCOS™ Knowledge Gap Instrument

## Purpose

Identify important missing, weak, contradictory, or unowned areas within the HCOS ecosystem.

## Questions

- What important question remains unanswered?
- Which affected perspectives are missing?
- Is evidence absent, narrow, outdated, or contradictory?
- Is a Standard missing a Method or Instrument?
- Is an Instrument operating without a clear governing Standard?
- Does a repository or artifact lack stewardship ownership?
- What harm or lost opportunity may result from leaving the gap unresolved?

## Output

- Gap statement
- Gap type
- Evidence and uncertainty
- Human and architectural significance
- Affected artifacts
- Recommended action
- Priority
- Proposed owner
- Review date
