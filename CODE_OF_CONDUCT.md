# HCOS™ Contributor Code of Conduct

HCOS™ is committed to creating a respectful, humane, and intellectually honest environment for collaboration.

## Expected Conduct

Contributors should:

- treat people with dignity
- assume good faith while remaining attentive to impact
- welcome questions and correction
- distinguish critique of ideas from judgment of people
- make room for lived experience and affected perspectives
- disclose uncertainty and conflicts of interest
- avoid harassment, humiliation, intimidation, or retaliation

## Human-Centered Disagreement

Healthy disagreement may reveal missing evidence, hidden assumptions, unequal power, or unrecognized consequences. Contributors should seek understanding before escalation and document unresolved disagreement when consensus is not appropriate.

## Accountability

Concerns should be addressed proportionally, fairly, and with attention to both protection and restoration. Serious safety, harassment, or integrity concerns may require removal of content or participation privileges.

## Guiding Principle

The quality of the knowledge ecosystem depends partly on the quality of the relationships through which knowledge is created.
