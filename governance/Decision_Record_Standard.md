# HCOS™ Architecture Decision Record Standard

## Purpose

This standard defines when and how Architecture Decision Records are used within HCOS.

## ADR Required When

A decision:

- changes ecosystem structure or classification
- introduces a new architectural category or major repository
- changes terminology with broad downstream impact
- creates, revises, or retires a Foundation or major Standard
- establishes significant governance authority
- accepts a consequential tradeoff or unresolved risk

## Required Fields

- Decision ID
- Title
- Date
- Status
- Context
- Problem Statement
- Options Considered
- Decision
- Rationale
- Supporting Evidence
- Assumptions and Limitations
- Human Impact
- Affected Documents
- Review Date
- Revision History

## Statuses

- Proposed
- Accepted
- Superseded
- Rejected
- Deprecated

## Principle

ADRs preserve reasoning. They do not replace changelogs, which record changes, or policies, which establish ongoing requirements.
