# HCOS™ Classification Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes how new HCOS work is assigned an architectural category.

## Primary Classification Question

> What is the artifact's primary function?

## Categories

- **Foundation:** Defines an enduring belief or principle.
- **Discipline:** Defines a major domain of HCOS knowledge and practice.
- **Standard:** Establishes expectations for responsible practice.
- **Method:** Defines a repeatable process.
- **Instrument:** Provides a practical tool for applying or evaluating work.
- **Research:** Generates or synthesizes evidence.
- **Insight:** Captures an emerging observation or interpretation.
- **Learning Resource:** Helps people understand or apply HCOS.
- **Example:** Demonstrates application without establishing authority.
- **Governance:** Controls decisions, classification, lifecycle, authority, or integrity.
- **Stewardship:** Sustains, improves, preserves, or reviews the ecosystem over time.

## Classification Rules

1. Classify by function rather than file format.
2. Use one primary category and identify secondary relationships separately.
3. Do not classify an Insight as a Standard merely because its argument is persuasive.
4. Revise an existing artifact when that creates greater coherence than adding a new one.
5. Document uncertainty when classification remains provisional.

## Reclassification

Artifacts may be reclassified when their purpose or maturity changes. Reclassification should preserve history, update relationships, and document rationale.
