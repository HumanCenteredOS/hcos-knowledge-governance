# HCOS™ Review Standard

## Purpose

This standard defines expectations for reviewing HCOS artifacts before approval or significant revision.

## Review Dimensions

### Human Impact

Does the artifact protect dignity, agency, safety, access, and meaningful participation?

### Evidence

Are claims supported appropriately? Are provenance, uncertainty, and contradictions visible?

### Architecture

Is the classification correct? Does the artifact duplicate, conflict with, or depend on existing work?

### Clarity

Can the intended audience understand purpose, scope, requirements, limitations, and next steps?

### Implementability

Can the artifact be applied without requiring hidden knowledge or unsupported authority?

### Stewardship

Are ownership, review timing, maintenance, and retirement considerations addressed?

## Proportional Review Levels

- **Lightweight:** Minor corrections and non-substantive improvements.
- **Standard:** New or materially revised routine artifacts.
- **Enhanced:** Standards, governance policies, high-impact Instruments, or cross-repository changes.
- **Foundational:** Foundations, major architectural changes, or decisions with broad human consequence.

## Review Outcome

A review may result in:

- approve
- approve with minor revisions
- return for revision
- request more evidence
- defer pending dependency resolution
- reject with documented rationale
