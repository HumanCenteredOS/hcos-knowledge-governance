# HCOS™ Document Lifecycle

## Purpose

This policy defines the formal status of HCOS documents from concept through archival preservation.

## Statuses

- **Concept:** Early idea not yet developed.
- **Draft:** Work in active development.
- **Under Review:** Submitted for defined review.
- **Approved:** Governance review is complete.
- **Published:** Released for intended use.
- **Maintained:** Actively supported and periodically reviewed.
- **Deprecated:** Still available but no longer recommended for new use.
- **Superseded:** Replaced by a newer artifact.
- **Archived:** Preserved for historical or traceability purposes.

## Status Requirements

Every document should display status and version metadata.

Transitions should identify:

- decision authority
- review evidence
- effective date
- affected relationships
- next review date when applicable

## Retirement

Retirement should preserve links, explain replacement guidance, and avoid silent deletion when an artifact has been publicly referenced.
