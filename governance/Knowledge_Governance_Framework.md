# HCOS™ Knowledge Governance Operating Framework

## Purpose

This framework translates HCOS Knowledge Governance architecture into operational responsibilities and decision practices.

## Governance Functions

- classify new artifacts
- determine review depth
- evaluate evidence and uncertainty
- document decision authority and rationale
- maintain version and release integrity
- manage dependencies and cross-repository impacts
- approve, revise, supersede, deprecate, or archive artifacts

## Decision Principles

Governance decisions should be:

- human-centered
- evidence-informed
- transparent
- proportional
- accountable
- revisable
- attentive to affected perspectives

## Minimum Decision Record

Significant decisions should identify:

- the issue
- decision authority
- evidence reviewed
- assumptions and uncertainty
- affected documents and people
- rationale
- implementation consequences
- review date

## Escalation

Escalate review when a proposal:

- creates or changes a Foundation or Standard
- affects multiple repositories
- establishes new authority or responsibility
- may materially affect safety, dignity, access, or rights
- conflicts with existing architecture
- relies on uncertain or contested evidence

## Stewardship Handoff

Approved decisions should include ongoing ownership, review timing, and criteria for revision or retirement.
