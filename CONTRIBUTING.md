# Contributing to HCOS™ Knowledge Governance

Thank you for helping strengthen the Human-Centered Operating Systems™ ecosystem.

## Before Contributing

Review:

- the repository [README](README.md)
- [HCOS™ Architecture Principles](architecture/HCOS_Architecture_Principles.md)
- [Classification Policy](governance/Classification_Policy.md)
- [Review Standard](governance/REVIEW_STANDARD.md)

## Contribution Expectations

Contributions should:

- protect human dignity
- be truthful about evidence and uncertainty
- avoid invented authority or unsupported claims
- distinguish observation from established principle
- identify affected people and potential harms
- preserve compatibility where reasonably possible
- explain relationships to existing HCOS work

## Proposed Changes

Use the [Change Proposal Template](templates/Change_Proposal_Template.md) for substantial additions or revisions.

Use an [Architecture Decision Record](templates/Architecture_Decision_Record.md) when a decision changes ecosystem structure, classification, terminology, governance, or long-term dependencies.

## Review

Review depth should be proportional to consequence. Minor wording corrections may receive lightweight review. New Foundations, Standards, governance requirements, and high-impact Instruments require deeper evidence and architecture review.

## Attribution and Licensing

Only contribute material you are authorized to share. Clearly identify third-party sources and preserve applicable attribution requirements.

## Conduct

Contributors are expected to engage with humility, respect, patience, and care. Disagreement should be used to improve understanding, not diminish people.
