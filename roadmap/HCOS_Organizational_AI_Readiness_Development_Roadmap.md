```text
Enduring Principle
        ↓
Organizational Readiness Standard
        ↓

Assessment Architecture
        ↓
AI-Specific Readiness Instrument
        ↓

        ↓
```

The technology-independent Standard and Method should remain architecturally prior to the AI-specific Instrument.
