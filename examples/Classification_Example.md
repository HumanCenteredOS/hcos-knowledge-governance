# Classification Example — Retrieval to Knowledge Stewardship

## Proposed Artifact

An analysis arguing that AI systems are moving from simple retrieval toward representations that preserve identity, provenance, history, and relationships.

## Primary Function

Interpret an emerging architectural shift and identify its significance for HCOS.

## Classification

**Primary:** Insight  
**Secondary Relationships:** Human-Centered AI, Knowledge Stewardship, Governance  
**Evidence Maturity:** Emerging Pattern

## Why It Is Not Yet a Standard

The artifact interprets evidence but does not establish sufficiently validated practice requirements.

## Recommended Placement

An HCOS Insights collection linked to the Human-Centered AI Discipline and the Knowledge Governance repository.

## Future Opportunities

- AI Knowledge Stewardship Standard
- Provenance Method
- Knowledge Architecture Assessment Instrument
