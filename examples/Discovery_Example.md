# Discovery Example — Multi-Stage AI Security Failure

## Source Observation

An AI-enabled attack combined multiple individually limited weaknesses, leaked credentials, infrastructure knowledge, and remote code execution into a successful objective.

## Systems Lesson

Risk emerged from composition across components rather than from one isolated vulnerability.

## Human and Organizational Lesson

Teams may underestimate danger when responsibility, visibility, and authority are fragmented across systems.

## Emerging Principle

> Security increasingly depends on governed relationships among components, identities, permissions, and actions—not only the safety of each component in isolation.

## HCOS Domains

- Governance
- Reliability and Safety
- Identity, Authority, and Delegation
- Workflow Stewardship
- Resource Stewardship

## Evidence Maturity

Emerging Pattern. Additional cross-domain evidence is needed before establishing a broad Standard.

## Recommended Next Action

Include the observation in a multi-source Architecture Synthesis focused on compositional risk and governed trust boundaries.
