# Governance Workflow Example — New HCOS Standard Proposal

## Proposal

A contributor proposes an AI Resource Stewardship Standard after reviewing reports of rapidly increasing model and infrastructure consumption.

## Workflow

1. **Discovery:** Identify the systems and human lessons.
2. **Classification:** Classify the initial analysis as an Insight.
3. **Synthesis:** Compare it with additional evidence across cost, capacity, sustainability, access, and workload.
4. **Evidence Maturity:** Determine whether the concept is an Emerging Pattern or Developing Principle.
5. **Evolution:** Decide whether to revise an existing Standard or create a new one.
6. **Governance Review:** Evaluate evidence, scope, duplication, affected people, and dependencies.
7. **Decision Record:** Document the architectural choice and alternatives.
8. **Publication:** Version and release the approved artifact.
9. **Stewardship:** Monitor implementation evidence and schedule review.

## Key Lesson

A compelling article may initiate the process, but it should not bypass synthesis, evidence maturity, or governance.
