# Synthesis Example — Emerging Human-Centered AI Pillars

## Evidence Body

Multiple Insights concerning AI workflow automation, infrastructure consumption, agent security, memory, provenance, and human escalation.

## Recurring Themes

- Knowledge Stewardship
- Workflow Stewardship
- Resource Stewardship
- Governance
- Reliability and Safety
- Human Judgment and Escalation

## Emerging Synthesis

The evidence suggests that Human-Centered AI requires governance across the entire lifecycle of knowledge, work, authority, resources, and recovery—not merely model performance.

## Maturity

Developing Principle. The pattern appears across several independent contexts but requires broader implementation validation.

## Architectural Recommendation

Strengthen D-002 Human-Centered AI with explicit pillars for Knowledge, Workflow, Resource, Governance, Reliability, and Human Stewardship.

## Contradiction

Greater autonomy may reduce routine coordination load while increasing the need for authority boundaries, auditability, and safe interruption.

## Roadmap

1. Draft pillar definitions.
2. Map existing HCOS artifacts to each pillar.
3. Identify missing Standards and Instruments.
4. Validate across healthcare and non-healthcare contexts.
